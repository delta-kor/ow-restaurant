# Customer types

## [hero Soldier: 76] {{c:soldier76}}

Orders **one dish**.\
Serve the requested dish before their waiting time runs out.

## [hero Roadhog] {{c:roadhog}}

Places [**two orders in succession**](#mechanic).\
They stay after the first serving, so prepare the second order too.\
Both orders contribute to the score: [**completing both earns more points than serving a {{c:soldier76}}**](#advantage).

## [hero Tracer] {{c:tracer}}

[**Waits less time than other customers.**](#penalty)\
They also arrive and move quickly. Serve them before orders pile up.

## [hero Bastion] {{c:bastion}}

[**Sensitive to serving order.**](#penalty)\
If you serve someone who ordered after them first, their order fails and they leave.

## [hero Hanzo] {{c:hanzo}}

Quick service or a freshly finished dish [**brings forward the arrival of the next two customers.**](#mechanic)\
Prepare upcoming orders before earning their praise.

## [hero Zarya] {{c:zarya}}

Orders [**three of the same dish**](#mechanic).\
All plates share one waiting period, so have the first plate ready quickly.\
[**Completing every plate earns more points than serving a {{c:soldier76}}.**](#advantage)

## [hero Mercy] {{c:mercy}}

Before entering, [**announces their table number and dish in a reservation message.**](#mechanic)\
Their wait after sitting down is short. Read the message and prepare in advance.

## [hero Hazard] {{c:hazard}}

[**Orders side dishes instead of main dishes.**](#mechanic)\
Burnt food can be ordered too. Check the order before discarding food you would normally throw away.

## [hero LifeWeaver] {{c:lifeweaver}}

[**Orders special dishes**](#mechanic) and [**supplies the extra ingredients needed.**](#mechanic)

- Orders with supplied ingredients grant [**20 extra seconds of waiting time.**](#advantage)
- Cook with the ingredients they provide.

## [hero Freja] {{c:freja}}

[**Sits upstairs if a seat is available.**](#mechanic)\
Otherwise, uses a regular seat.

- [**{{i:17}} cannot serve upstairs.**](#penalty)\
  Carry the food there yourself.
- {{i:17}} can serve them at a regular seat.

## [hero Mauga] {{c:mauga}}

[**Sensitive to serving order.**](#penalty)

- If you serve someone who ordered after them first, their order fails and they leave.
- A failed order [**stuns all players for five seconds.**](#penalty)

## [hero D.Va] {{c:dva}}

Places [**two orders in succession**](#mechanic) and is [**sensitive to serving order.**](#penalty)

- If you serve someone who ordered after them first, their order fails and they leave.
- Quick service or a freshly finished dish [**brings forward the arrival of the next four customers.**](#mechanic)
- Both orders contribute to the score: [**completing both earns more points than serving a {{c:soldier76}}**](#advantage).
- A failed order causes [**three times the usual loss of satisfaction and stage settlement points**](#penalty).

## [hero Kiriko] {{c:kiriko}}

[**Teleports in and out**](#mechanic) and has a short waiting time.

- Successful service adds [**50 bonus points to the total score.**](#advantage)
- A failed order does not cause the usual satisfaction loss.

## [hero Jetpack Cat] {{c:jetpackcat}}

[**Waits at the restaurant entrance**](#mechanic) instead of sitting down.\
Carry the requested dish to the delivery location yourself.

- [**{{i:17}} cannot make the delivery.**](#penalty)
- Other customers keep arriving during delivery. Splitting kitchen and delivery duties helps.

## [hero Domina] {{c:domina}}

[**Accepts only properly finished dishes.**](#penalty)\
Even the correct dish fails if it has lost freshness or is not fully cooked.\
Serve it as soon as it is finished.

- [**{{i:11}} restores a dish's freshness.**](#advantage)

## [hero Reinhardt] {{c:reinhardt}}

Orders [**nine of the same dish**](#mechanic).

- All plates share one waiting period, so have the first plate ready quickly.
- [**Completing every plate earns more points than serving a {{c:soldier76}}.**](#advantage)
- [**Every plate guarantees a tip.**](#advantage)
- [**Failure causes a massive nine times the usual loss of satisfaction and stage settlement points.**](#penalty)

## [hero Junker Queen] {{c:junkerqueen}}

[**Sensitive to serving order.**](#penalty)

- If you serve someone who ordered after them first, their order fails and they leave.
- A failed order [**stuns players within 10 m for 15 seconds.**](#penalty)
- Other customers waiting within 10 m also [**fail their orders and leave**](#penalty). Give this customer priority.

## [hero Sombra] {{c:sombra}}

Before entering, [**announces their table number and dish in a reservation message.**](#mechanic)

- After sitting down, [**the order is hidden as ???**](#penalty). Remember it beforehand.
- On failure, [**for 20 seconds**](#penalty), players' movement speed setting drops to 50%, and special tools and movement boots cannot be used.
- During that time, the fryer, grill and pan cook at half speed. Food newly placed in a pot takes up to five extra seconds.
- [**{{i:17}} also disappears**](#penalty) and does not automatically return when the effect ends.

## [hero Sigma] {{c:sigma}}

[**The remaining waiting time is hidden, so you cannot tell how long they will wait.**](#penalty)\
At random, [**all waiting customers become confused**](#penalty).

- Most customers [**are stunned for one second, then choose a new order.**](#penalty)\
  Their waiting time also restarts.
- [**{{c:zarya}} and {{c:reinhardt}} fail their orders and leave.**](#penalty)
- {{c:sombra}}'s hidden order is revealed.

## [hero Moira] {{c:moira}}

Places [**four orders in succession**](#mechanic) and is [**sensitive to serving order.**](#penalty)

- If you serve someone who ordered after them first, their order fails and they leave.
- [**All four orders contribute to the score, earning especially many points when completed.**](#advantage)
- Even one failed order [**ends the service.**](#penalty)
- Successful service may earn [**a random tool or pair of boots**](#advantage) as a tip.

## [hero Ramattra] {{c:ramattra}}

Places [**two orders in succession**](#mechanic) and is [**sensitive to serving order.**](#penalty)

- If you serve someone who ordered after them first, their order fails and they leave.
- [**The remaining waiting time is hidden, so you cannot tell how long they will wait.**](#penalty)
- Both orders contribute to the score: [**completing both earns more points than serving a {{c:soldier76}}**](#advantage).
- Successful service may earn [**a random tool or pair of boots**](#advantage) as a tip.
- On failure, [**for 20 seconds**](#penalty), players' movement speed setting drops to 50%, and special tools and movement boots cannot be used.
- During that time, the fryer, grill and pan cook at half speed. Food newly placed in a pot takes up to five extra seconds.
- [**{{i:17}} also disappears**](#penalty) and does not automatically return when the effect ends.
- [**Up to 30 burnt items**](#penalty) appear nearby.\
  No more appear once the kitchen has 32 items in total.
