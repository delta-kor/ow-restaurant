# Items

## [item 8]

[**Increases movement and cutting-board chopping speed for 100 seconds.**](#advantage)

- Movement speed is set to 150% of the base value.
- Chopping at a cutting board is twice as fast.

## [item 9]

[**Greatly boosts the team's movement, chopping and cooking while slowing the surrounding world.**](#advantage)

- [**The team's knife durability is temporarily multiplied by 10, increasing chopping progress per strike by up to 5.5 times.**](#advantage) Knives with more durability gain a larger boost.
- [**Pan, fryer and grill cooking power becomes 11 times the default.**](#advantage) The oven and ice maker are also boosted in {{t:1}}.
- [**Food newly placed in a pot finishes up to 10 seconds sooner.**](#advantage) Food already cooking is unaffected.
- [**{{i:16}} and {{i:20}} produce 10 times the cooking progress per activation.**](#advantage)
- [**Time runs at 30% speed and every player's movement speed setting rises to 280%.**](#advantage)
- [**Knives cannot be changed or dropped during the boost.**](#penalty)
- When the effect ends, movement and cooking power return to normal, and [**all knife durability is restored to 100%.**](#advantage)

## [item 11]

Use while holding food to [**restore its freshness.**](#advantage)

- Use just before serving {{c:domina}}, who demands properly finished dishes.

## [item 12]

With empty hands, [**instantly removes the nearby item you are looking at.**](#mechanic)

- Clears unwanted ingredients and burnt food.
- [**Food also disappears, so take care around ingredients needed for orders.**](#penalty)
- Durability drains while the button is held.\
  It is consumed even without a target.

## [item 15]

[**Creates one extra copy of the food in your hand.**](#advantage)

- The copy inherits the original's durability and cooking progress.
- The original remains, making it useful for preparing several identical dishes.
- Knives, tools, movement gear and cash cannot be copied.

## [item 16]

With empty hands, [**grills the nearby ingredient you are looking at without using the grill.**](#advantage)

- In {{t:1}}, this performs oven cooking instead.
- Stop when cooking finishes: continued heating may advance the food to another stage or burn it.
- Holding the button repeats activation and consumes durability even without a target.

## [item 17]

[**Summons {{i:17}} to help serve dishes.**](#advantage)

- Collects food near the designated kitchen location and serves a matching order.
- If several customers ordered the same dish, selects the first matching order.
- Cannot serve {{c:freja}} upstairs or {{c:jetpackcat}}.
- Disappears when service ends or it is hacked.

## [item 19]

[**Produces three ingredient packs for the current cuisine category.**](#advantage)

- Available in {{t:0}}.
- Cut the packs open with a knife.

## [item 20]

With empty hands, [**freezes the nearby ingredient you are looking at without using the ice maker.**](#advantage)

- Available in {{t:1}}.
- Holding the button repeats activation and consumes durability even without a target.

## [item 10]

Press jump again in midair to [**jump higher once more.**](#advantage)

- Release jump once before pressing it again in midair.
- You can repeat this before landing by releasing and pressing again.
- Useful for reaching the upstairs seats.

## [item 13]

[**Dashes quickly in the direction you are looking.**](#advantage)

- Combines a leap with forward movement for fast travel.

## [item 14]

Use while holding an ordered dish to [**teleport to that customer's table.**](#advantage)

- If several customers ordered the same dish, moves to the first matching table.
- If no order matches, moves onto the main cutting board.
- The food in your hand travels with you.
- Durability is consumed even if no order matches.

## [item 1]

The basic knife for chopping ingredients at a cutting board.\
The other knives also follow these common rules.

- Place ingredients on a cutting board, look at them and press interact to chop.\
  Hold the button to keep chopping.
- Several ingredients on the board can be chopped together; each ingredient consumes durability.
- [**Lower durability reduces chopping progress per strike.**](#penalty) The knife breaks when durability runs out.
- Makes less progress per strike and wears out faster than other knives.

## [item 6]

Compared with {{i:1}}, [**makes more progress per strike and has better durability.**](#advantage)

## [item 2]

Makes the same progress per strike as {{i:6}}, but [**has better durability and chops 1.25 times as fast at a cutting board.**](#advantage)

## [item 3]

[**Can chop ingredients directly in your hand.**](#mechanic)

- Hold an ingredient and hold interact to keep chopping.\
  Can also be used at a cutting board.
- Progress per strike and durability match {{i:2}}.\
  Cutting-board chopping speed matches a regular knife.

## [item 4]

[**Makes substantial progress per strike and has strong durability.**](#advantage)

- Makes twice the progress per strike of {{i:6}}, with the same durability as {{i:2}}.

## [item 5]

[**Quickly repeat chopping at a cutting board by pressing the button again.**](#advantage)

- Tap interact repeatedly to restart chopping sooner than with a regular knife.\
  Holding the button has the same continuous chopping interval as a regular knife.
- Progress per strike matches {{i:1}}; durability matches {{i:6}}.

## [item 7]

[**A legendary knife that chops ingredients directly in your hand.**](#mechanic)

- [**Can chop most ingredients in one strike and has the strongest durability.**](#advantage)
- Hold an ingredient and hold interact to keep chopping.\
  Can also be used at a cutting board.
- Can be purchased directly from the shop, but **can also be made by combining {{i:4}} and {{i:3}}.**

## [item 18]

Cash that customers may randomly drop.\
Pick it up and press interact to [**receive $100.**](#advantage)

- Used to buy knives, items and upgrades at the shop.
- Serving cash to a customer [**confuses them and makes them choose a new order.**](#mechanic)
- [**{{c:sombra}} chooses a new order, revealing its contents.**](#advantage)
- [**{{c:zarya}} and {{c:reinhardt}} cannot be confused, so their orders fail.**](#penalty)
