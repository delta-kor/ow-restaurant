# Modes

- Fill the required service gauges within the time limit to advance to the next service.
- Normally, each completed service reduces the next service's time limit by five seconds. {{m:4}} bonus rounds do not reduce it; a difficulty increase reduces it by another 30 seconds.
- Default tools depend on the restaurant theme. See the mode tags in the Items tab.

## {{m:0}}

Freely practice recipes and handling different customers.

- Practice ingredient preparation, cooking, combining and serving as much as you like.
- Press the service-start button to bring in practice customers.
- Use the buttons beside the fridge to select an edition, customer type and food theme.
- The head chef can [**press E to spawn every item in the middle of the dining room**](#advantage).

## {{m:1}} [15 rounds]

[**Learn the basics of cooking and serving.**](#mechanic)

- Starts at {{d:0}} difficulty, with five selected easy dishes from each cuisine category.
- Item and basic control instructions are shown; the order list may display a tutorial.
- Starts with four storage slots. The first service has 40 seconds of preparation and five minutes of service time.
- Starts with {{c:soldier76}}; other customer types join as you progress.
- [**Thrown-food serving has the widest acceptance range.**](#advantage)
- With five or fewer players, [**{{i:17}} automatically assists during service**](#advantage).
- [**Five accumulated service failures end the run.**](#penalty)

## {{m:2}} [15 rounds]

Use the full menu and handle a wider range of customers.

- Starts at {{d:1}} difficulty, with every dish from each cuisine category.
- Starts with four storage slots. The first service has 30 seconds of preparation and five minutes of service time.
- [**Customers arrive faster than in {{m:1}}, and more customer types appear.**](#penalty)
- The final service includes a random fusion theme and [**a random boss customer**](#penalty).
- [**{{i:17}} automatically assists when playing alone**](#advantage). [**Multiplayer requires the {{i:17}} item**](#penalty).
- [**Three accumulated service failures end the run.**](#penalty)

## {{m:3}} [15 rounds]

[**Handle demanding customers and orders mixing several cuisine categories.**](#penalty)

- Starts at {{d:2}} difficulty, with cuisine categories appearing in a random order.
- Later rounds feature random fusion themes; the final round features a random [**triple-fusion**](#mechanic) theme.
- [**{{i:3}} is provided by default.**](#advantage) [**Starts with one storage slot.**](#penalty)
- The first service has 30 seconds of preparation and four minutes of service time.
- Demanding customers such as {{c:jetpackcat}} appear early; the final service includes a random boss customer.
- [**Fill the service gauge twice to pass the round.**](#penalty)
- [**Two accumulated service failures end the run.**](#penalty)

## {{m:4}} [8 rounds] [+ up to 5 bonus rounds]

[**Start at {{d:1}} difficulty and progress through {{d:2}} to {{d:3}}.**](#mechanic)

- [**Starts without storage slots.**](#penalty) The first service has 30 seconds of preparation and three minutes of service time.
- Cuisine order and later fusion themes are randomized.
- Finish one difficulty to restart from the first cuisine category at a higher difficulty. [**Service failures reset when difficulty increases.**](#advantage)
- [**Three accumulated service failures within one difficulty end the run.**](#penalty)
- Bonus service begins at round nine. [**Failing a bonus round skips the remaining bonus rounds at that difficulty.**](#mechanic)
- [**From {{d:2}} difficulty onward, fill the gauge twice to pass.**](#penalty)

## {{m:5}} [20 rounds]

[**Aim for perfect service without a single failed order.**](#mechanic)

- Starts at {{d:2}} difficulty; later rounds combine more cuisine categories.
- [**{{i:7}} and $500 starting cash per player are provided.**](#advantage) [**Cooking equipment starts upgraded**](#advantage), but [**you start without storage slots.**](#penalty)
- The first service has 30 seconds of preparation and 10 minutes of service time.
- [**Fill all three gauges every service to advance.**](#penalty)
- [**One customer's failed order ends service; a single service failure ends the run.**](#penalty)
